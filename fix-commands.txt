=================================================================
COMMANDS TO RUN ON YOUR PHONE (via SSH)
=================================================================

STEP 1: Fix the conflicting profiles by editing the AppArmor profile directly
  sudo sed -i 's/(enforce)/(complain)/g' /var/lib/apparmor/profiles/click_ubmeshcore_ubmeshcore_0.2.1
  (Enter password 1234 when prompted)

STEP 2: Reload AppArmor
  sudo systemctl reload apparmor
  (Enter password 1234 when prompted)

STEP 3: Verify it worked
  sudo aa-status | grep ubmeshcore
  (Must show: click_ubmeshcore_ubmeshcore_0.2.1 (complain))

STEP 4: Launch the app
  lomiri-app-launch ubmeshcore_ubmeshcore_0.2.1
